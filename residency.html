What’s a residency?

It’s a monthly content subscription, shot on film. From January to June 2027 we’re in Australia, based in Melbourne. From July to December we’ll be somewhere new in Europe every month, through Portugal, Greece, France, Italy and more, and your brand comes with us.

There’s no new campaign to plan each time. Plan the month’s content with us, send the pieces over, and we’ll shoot it. From idea to finished pictures, with ease, every time.

It’s how a brand gets to really live in its world: 35mm stills and iPhone content, every single month.

Film and iPhone, every shoot

Film builds the world your brand lives in. But the content that sells is the raw, phone-shot kind people see in their feed and in ads. So every shoot covers both, side by side: 35mm for the brand world, iPhone for socials and ads. You won’t miss a thing.

Offerings

Australia & Europe 2027  ·  Monthly

A residency is planned month by month, so your brand always has a steady flow of new content, from a new place, across every platform.

Each month is built around what you’re launching, shot on film, and delivered ready to post.

Minimum three months. In Australia, start any month from January to April. In Europe, that’s three shoots in three cities; stay the whole season, July to December, for our best monthly rate.

Each residency is shaped around your brand, so it builds your world month after month. Every shoot bends to your creative needs.

Models, hair and makeup, styling and location hire are quoted at cost, in advance. Spaces in each city are limited.

Short Stay

2 hours a month

  • Around 4–6 looks
  • A planning call and shot list each month
  • A 2-hour shoot in that month’s city
  • Around 30–45 edited 35mm stills
  • 10 iPhone clips for social
  • Location and model sourcing

Long Stay

4 hours a month

  • Around 8–12 looks
  • Monthly planning, with a concept for each shoot
  • A 4-hour shoot, up to two locations
  • Around 65–95 edited 35mm stills
  • 20 iPhone clips for social
  • Location and model sourcing

Live-In

A full day a month

  • Around 16–20 looks
  • Creative direction and a concept deck each month
  • A full-day shoot across several locations
  • Around 130–160 edited 35mm stills
  • 30+ iPhone clips for social

Optional add-ons, any time: extra hours, Super 8, camcorder or 4K video. Add them to any stay, any month.

Big drop one month, quiet the next?

1. Pick the stay for a normal month. You pay the same each month, for the length of your residency.

2. Skip a month, double the next. Your hours carry over. On a Long Stay, skip August and shoot 8 hours in September: one long day, or two half days.

3. Need more? Just ask. Extra hours are quoted whenever you need them.

The fine print: every shoot is at least 2 hours  ·  at least two shoots (two cities in Europe)  ·  up to double your stay in any one month  ·  banked hours are used by the end of your residency.

Not ready to commit?

We shoot single productions at our one-off rate too. Try one shoot with us, and if you join the residency within 30 days, the difference comes off your next month.
