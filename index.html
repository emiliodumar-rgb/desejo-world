About

Founded by Emilio Dümar, Melbourne-based photographer.

Deséjo began on the salt-washed coastline of Australia. What started as a personal approach to image-making has grown into a full-scale production studio working with brands globally.

At its core, Deséjo is built on one thing. Creating work that feels real.
